What is domain investing? A beginner guide to the Indonesian market

By the Djurnal.id editors · November 16, 2026

This is a sample article skeleton. Replace it with your own writing and sources.

The short version

Domain investing means buying domain names in the hope of reselling or leasing them at a higher price.

Key terms

  • Aftermarket: the market for domains that are already registered.
  • Backorder: reserving a domain so it is bought automatically when released.
  • Escrow: a third party that holds the money until the domain transfers.

Risks to know

Yearly renewal costs, difficulty finding buyers, and trademark disputes.

Conclusion

Start with research and a small budget. This is not financial advice.
